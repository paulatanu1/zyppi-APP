# Zyppi Delivery Partner Agreement

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This agreement is between **[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]** (CIN [CIN], registered office [REGISTERED ADDRESS]) ("Zyppi") and the person registering in the Zyppi delivery partner app ("Delivery Partner", "you").

You accept it by:
1. ticking "I agree";
2. drawing your signature; and
3. confirming with the OTP sent to your mobile number.

That electronic acceptance is a valid and binding contract under section 10A of the Information Technology Act, 2000.

## 1. Your relationship with Zyppi
1.1 You are an **independent delivery partner, not an employee** of Zyppi. You choose when to go online and which orders to accept.
1.2 Zyppi will help you register under any social-security scheme for gig and platform workers that applies to you, such as the Code on Social Security, 2020, and e-Shram.

## 2. Joining
2.1 You must be at least 18. You must provide true details: Aadhaar, a selfie, and bank or UPI details. For a motor vehicle you also need a valid driving licence, the vehicle registration (RC) and valid insurance.
2.2 Zyppi verifies your details and may approve your application, reject it, ask for changes, or re-verify at any time.
2.3 Your account is personal. Do not let anyone else use it, or deliver for you.

## 3. Doing deliveries
3.1 **Location.** While you are online or on a delivery, the app collects your **precise location, including in the background**. It is used to offer orders, guide you, show customers where their order is, calculate your pay and keep you safe. Your location is not collected when you are offline.
3.2 **Pickup.** At each store, show the store the pickup code (OTP) in your app and collect only the items listed. One order may need pickups from more than one store.
3.3 **Delivery.** Hand the order only to the customer, after the customer tells you the 4-digit delivery code. Never ask for or accept the code in any other way.
3.4 You must not use fake-GPS apps or any trick to change your location. The app detects mocked locations and refuses them, and using one may lead to suspension.
3.5 Follow traffic laws, wear a helmet, and do not ride under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Do not open, swap or damage packages.
3.6 Be polite to customers, store staff and other riders. Harassment or abuse leads to removal.
3.7 Use **SOS** in the app in an emergency. Zyppi's team is alerted with your location.

## 4. Cash on delivery
4.1 Cash you collect for an order belongs to Zyppi or the seller. The app keeps a running balance of what you hold.
4.2 Hand the cash over at the store or as instructed, within the time shown in the app. Zyppi may deduct any unsettled cash from your payouts.

## 5. Pay
5.1 Your pay for each delivery follows the rate card shown in the app (distance, extra pickups, waiting time, peak and incentive rewards). Customer tips are passed to you in full.
5.2 You can request a payout of your balance (minimum ₹100) from the app. It is paid to your bank account or UPI ID, and the reference is recorded.
5.3 Zyppi may correct mistakes in your earnings with a written reason shown in the app. Zyppi may also deduct, with reasons, for lost or damaged orders that were your fault.
5.4 You are responsible for your own income tax. Zyppi deducts tax at source where the law requires it.

## 6. Suspension and ending
6.1 You can stop delivering at any time. Settle any cash you hold first.
6.2 Zyppi may suspend or deactivate your account for:
- false documents;
- fraud, including fake GPS;
- theft or tampering with orders;
- unsafe riding;
- misconduct;
- repeated customer complaints.

Before a permanent deactivation, you will be told why and can respond.

## 7. Data
7.1 Use customers' names, phone numbers and addresses only to complete their delivery. Do not save, share or contact customers for anything else.
7.2 How Zyppi handles your data is explained in the Privacy Policy.

## 8. Responsibility
8.1 You are responsible for your vehicle, its insurance and any fines. Zyppi is not liable for accidents or losses that were not caused by Zyppi, except where the law says otherwise or where Zyppi provides insurance cover.
8.2 You will compensate Zyppi for orders or cash lost through your fault.

## 9. Changes
Zyppi may update this agreement or the rate card. You get notice in the app, and important changes need your fresh acceptance.

## 10. Grievances, law and courts
Contact Zyppi's Grievance Officer, **[GRIEVANCE OFFICER NAME]** ([GRIEVANCE EMAIL], [SUPPORT PHONE]). This agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Purulia, West Bengal, have exclusive jurisdiction.
