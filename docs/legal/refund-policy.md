# Zyppi Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy is part of the Zyppi Terms of Use. It applies to every order, whether the products are sold by Zyppi or by a marketplace Seller.

## 1. Cancelling an order
1.1 Contact support through the app to cancel an order before it is packed. Once it is out for delivery, it can usually no longer be cancelled.
1.2 We or the Seller may cancel an order, or an item in it, if the item is out of stock, mispriced because of a technical error, cannot be delivered to your address, or the order looks fraudulent. You will be refunded for anything cancelled.

## 2. Problems with your order
Use **Report a problem** on the order in the app **within 2 hours of delivery** to report missing, wrong, damaged, spoiled or expired items. Choose the items and quantities. Add 1 to 3 photos, unless the item is missing. Then choose what you would like back:
- a refund to your Zyppi Wallet (instant once approved);
- a refund to your original payment (UPI or card orders only); or
- a free replacement, where the item is in stock.

Our team reviews every report and may approve, reduce or reject it; we tell you the reason if we reject it. If a replacement isn't possible, we refund the item to your Zyppi Wallet instead. We may ask you to hand a wrong or damaged item back to the rider.

For a problem you find after the 2 hours, contact in-app support. We may then credit your Zyppi Wallet at our discretion.

## 3. What can't be returned
Unless it is wrong, damaged, defective or expired, the following can't be returned:
- products you've opened or used;
- perishables, frozen and chilled products;
- personal-care and hygiene products;
- medicines.

## 4. How refunds are paid
| You paid by | Refund goes to | Usually takes |
|---|---|---|
| UPI or card (PayU) | The same account or card, or your Zyppi Wallet if you choose it | 5–7 working days after we approve it (your bank may take longer); the wallet is instant |
| Cash on delivery | Your Zyppi Wallet | Instant once approved |
| Zyppi Wallet | Your Zyppi Wallet | Instant |

If an order was paid partly from your Zyppi Wallet, that part always goes back to your Zyppi Wallet.

A refund never exceeds what you paid for the item. It is the item price less that item's share of any coupon discount.

Delivery and handling fees are refunded when the whole order is cancelled by us or the Seller. They are also refunded when the whole order could not be delivered for a reason that wasn't your fault.

## 5. Zyppi Wallet
5.1 Your Zyppi Wallet holds store credit from refunds, approved problem reports and credits from Zyppi. You can use it on any Zyppi order, alone or together with UPI, card or cash on delivery.
5.2 It can't be topped up, transferred to someone else, or withdrawn as cash or to a bank account.
5.3 Refunded money in your wallet does not expire.
5.4 If you delete your Zyppi account, any wallet balance is lost. The app warns you first.
5.5 When you pay with the wallet, we check your balance at the moment the order is placed. If it has changed since you opened checkout, we ask you to confirm the new amount.

## 6. If the rider can't deliver
If the order can't be delivered because nobody was available, the address was wrong, or the delivery code wasn't shared, we may cancel it. For prepaid orders we then refund the product value. We may keep the delivery fee and the cost of perishables that can't be restocked.

## 7. Coupons
If you cancel an order, a coupon used on it may not be reusable. If you return part of an order and the rest no longer meets the coupon's minimum, the refund is reduced by the coupon value.
