# Zyppi Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy explains how **[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]** ("Zyppi") collects and uses personal data, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000. It covers:
- the Zyppi customer app and website;
- the Zyppi delivery partner (rider) app;
- the Zyppi Marketplace seller app and website.

Zyppi is the Data Fiduciary.

## 1. What we collect
| Who | Data | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Everyone | Mobile number, name, email (optional), device and app version, IP address, crash and usage data | Sign-in, security and fraud prevention, support, improving the apps |
| Customers | Delivery addresses and their map location, orders, ratings, support chats, push token | Delivering orders, order updates, support, offers you agree to |
| Customers paying online | Payment status and reference from PayU (never your full card or bank details) | Confirming payments, refunds |
| Riders | Date of birth, address, Aadhaar (photos and last 4 digits), driving licence, vehicle RC, selfie, bank or UPI details. **Precise location while on duty, including in the background.** | Verification, assigning and tracking deliveries, safety (SOS), paying you |
| Sellers | Owner name, PAN, Aadhaar last 4 digits, GSTIN, FSSAI licence, bank or UPI details, live selfie, shop photos with their location and time, drawn signature, shop location and hours | Verification (KYC), legal compliance, listing your products, payouts, tax reporting |
| Signing an agreement | Your signature, the time, the version and fingerprint of the document, the phone number verified by OTP, IP address and device | Evidence that you agreed (IT Act 2000, s.10A) |

We do not collect more than we need. Riders' location is collected only while they are on duty.

## 2. Who we share it with
- **Riders** get the customer's name, phone number and delivery address for their delivery. **Sellers** get the order items. Neither keeps this data after the order.
- **Customers** see the rider's name and photo, the rider's live location during delivery, and the Seller's business details.
- **PayU** processes payments. **Google Firebase** (Google Cloud, India region) hosts our data, sign-in and notifications. **Google Maps** handles addresses and maps.
- **Authorities,** when the law requires it, for example for tax filings such as GST TCS and TDS on seller payouts.

We do not sell personal data.

## 3. How long we keep it
- **Account data:** while the account is open.
- **Orders, invoices and tax records:** 8 years (accounting and tax law).
- **KYC documents and signed agreements:** for the duration of the relationship plus 3 years. A longer period applies where the law requires it.
- **Location history of riders:** 90 days, unless it is needed for a dispute or safety incident.

## 4. Your rights
Under the DPDP Act you can:
- access a summary of your data;
- correct or update it;
- withdraw consent;
- have your data erased, unless we must keep it by law;
- nominate someone to act for you.

**Delete account** in the app removes your account and personal data. Orders are kept, anonymised, for accounting.

## 5. Security
Data is encrypted in transit. Access is limited by role, and KYC documents are never public. Every administrative change is logged.

## 6. Children
Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children without a parent's consent.

## 7. Changes
If we change this policy in an important way, we will ask you to accept the new version in the app.
