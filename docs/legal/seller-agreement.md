# Zyppi Marketplace Seller Agreement

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This agreement is between **[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]** (CIN [CIN], GSTIN [GSTIN], registered office [REGISTERED ADDRESS]) ("Zyppi") and the shop or business registering in the Zyppi Marketplace app or website (the "Seller", "you").

You accept it by:
1. ticking "I agree";
2. drawing your signature; and
3. confirming with the OTP sent to your registered mobile number.

That electronic acceptance is a valid and binding contract under section 10A of the Information Technology Act, 2000.

## 1. Joining
1.1 **What you must provide.** To sell on Zyppi you must give true and complete details:
- owner name, PAN and the last 4 digits of Aadhaar;
- GSTIN, where you are required to have one;
- an FSSAI licence or registration, for food;
- shop address and map location;
- bank or UPI details;
- a live selfie;
- photos of your shop taken at the shop.

1.2 Zyppi reviews every application. It may approve it, reject it or ask for changes. Zyppi may also re-verify your details at any time.
1.3 You must keep your licences valid. Your listings are paused automatically when your FSSAI licence expires, and they come back once you upload a valid one.
1.4 Logins you add for your staff act on your behalf. You are responsible for what they do.

## 2. How you work with Zyppi
You choose one of these models when you register. Zyppi must agree to any change.

**(a) Subscription.** You pay a subscription fee for the plan you choose (1, 3 or 6 months) and set your own selling price for each product.
- **Zyppi may add a platform add-on to your price.** The customer then pays your price plus the add-on.
- **You are paid your own price.** The add-on is Zyppi's fee for the platform, marketing and delivery.
- The seller app always shows both your price and the customer price.

**(b) Supply.** You supply products at an agreed supply price.
- Zyppi sets the customer price. **You are paid the agreed supply price** for each unit sold.
- The difference is Zyppi's.
- Unless agreed otherwise in writing, stock you keep in a Zyppi store stays yours until it is sold (consignment).

**Fulfilment.** Your stock is either at your own shop, where a Zyppi rider picks orders up, or in a Zyppi store. This is as agreed at registration.

## 3. Prices, discounts and MRP
3.1 No price may be above the product's MRP. That includes your price plus any add-on, and it is required by the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011. The app does not let you save a price above MRP.
3.2 **Special discounts** you create are paid for by you. They reduce both your payout and the customer price for their duration. Zyppi's add-on is not reduced.
3.3 Coupons, free delivery and other offers that Zyppi runs are paid for by Zyppi. They do not reduce your payout unless you agreed to share the cost in writing.
3.4 You must not change prices in a misleading way, for example raising a price just before a discount. Zyppi may limit how often prices can change.

## 4. Products
4.1 You may sell only products you are legally allowed to sell. They must be genuine, safe, within their expiry date, and correctly labelled (MRP, net quantity, manufacturer, FSSAI details for food).
4.2 You must not list prohibited items. These include:
- tobacco and alcohol, where not allowed;
- prescription medicines without the required licence;
- counterfeit goods;
- anything banned by law.
4.3 Product names, photos and descriptions must be accurate. New products and edits you submit go live after Zyppi reviews them.
4.4 You give Zyppi a free, non-exclusive licence to use the product content and images you upload on the Platform and in Zyppi's marketing. You confirm you have the right to give it.

## 5. Orders
5.1 When an order comes in, the seller app rings and shows the order.
- **Accept it within the time shown** (currently 5 minutes).
- **After you accept, you may not cancel the order.** You must pack it correctly and have it ready on time.
- If you can't fulfil an item, use "Report item issue". Zyppi support will then substitute or refund it.
5.2 **Hand over only to the assigned rider, and only after entering the pickup code (OTP)** the rider shows you. The handover is recorded with that code.
5.3 You must keep your stock levels on Zyppi accurate. If you sell an item in your shop, update it or mark it out of stock.
5.4 Missing acceptance times, frequent item issues or wrong items lower your ranking. Repeated failures may pause your listings, or lead to suspension.

## 6. Ranking and ratings
6.1 When several sellers offer the same product, Zyppi ranks the offers automatically. The ranking uses price, customer ratings, reliability (acceptance, cancellations, item issues) and availability. Zyppi explains these factors in the app, and does not favour any seller without labelling it.
6.2 Only customers with delivered orders can rate you. You must not rate yourself, buy reviews, or ask customers for ratings in exchange for anything.

## 7. Payments to you
7.1 For each delivered item, Zyppi credits your earnings with your price (subscription) or the supply price (supply), minus any special discount.
7.2 **Hold period.** Earnings are held for the return window (currently 7 days after delivery) and then become available.
7.3 **Deductions.** Zyppi may deduct from your earnings:
- unpaid subscription fees;
- refunds for items that were your fault (wrong, damaged, expired or missing items);
- tax collected at source under section 52 of the CGST Act, 2017, and tax deducted under section 194-O of the Income-tax Act, 1961, at the rates in force. These are deposited in your name and shown in your statement.
7.4 You can request a payout of your available balance from the app. Zyppi pays to your registered bank account or UPI ID and records the transaction reference (UTR). Payouts are normally made within 3 working days of the request.
7.5 Monthly statements of sales, discounts, fees, deductions and payouts are available in the app.
7.6 You are responsible for your own GST and income-tax returns. Invoices for your sales are issued in your name.

## 8. Subscription fees
8.1 The plan fee is due at the start of each period. It is deducted from your earnings, or you pay it by UPI or bank transfer to Zyppi.
8.2 You get reminders before your plan ends, then a grace period (currently 3 days). If it is still unpaid after that, your listings are hidden until you renew.
8.3 Fees already paid for a period are not refunded if you stop selling before the period ends, except where Zyppi suspends you without cause.

## 9. Suspension and termination
9.1 Either side may end this agreement with 15 days' written notice, given in the app or by email.
9.2 Zyppi may suspend or end it at once if you give false KYC, sell prohibited or unsafe products, commit fraud, misuse customer data, or seriously or repeatedly breach this agreement.
9.3 Orders already accepted must still be fulfilled. Your balance, after deductions and the hold period, is paid out.

## 10. Data and confidentiality
10.1 You may use customer details (name, items, order number) only to fulfil the order. You must not keep them, contact customers for any other purpose, or share them. This is also required by the DPDP Act, 2023.
10.2 Each side keeps the other's business information confidential.

## 11. Responsibility
11.1 You are responsible for your products and their compliance with the law. You will indemnify Zyppi against claims, penalties and losses caused by your products, your breach of this agreement or the law, or your staff.
11.2 Zyppi's total liability to you in any month is limited to the fees Zyppi earned from you in that month. Neither side is liable for indirect losses.

## 12. Changes
Zyppi may update this agreement with 15 days' notice in the app. Fee or add-on changes apply only to orders after the notice ends. If you do not agree, you may stop selling before the change takes effect. Important changes need your fresh acceptance in the app.
